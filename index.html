OUR STORY

A quiet room with the doors left open to the morning breeze.

We started AMARA in 2022 with a simple obsession: fresh roasted coffee, good warm bread, and a space where nobody rushes you.

Our beans come directly from family-run estates in Chikmagalur and Coorg. We roast in five-kilo batches on Tuesday and Friday mornings, bake our laminated sourdough croissants before sunrise, and grind every shot to order.

No loud speakers, no pre-ground beans, and no hurried tables. Pull up a cane chair, order a cortado, and stay as long as you like.
